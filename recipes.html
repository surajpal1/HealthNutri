Healthy Recipes 🥗

Simple and nutritious meals for your daily health goals.

🥣

Protein Oats

Oats with milk, banana and peanut butter. A simple high-energy breakfast.

🔥 450 kcal 💪 18g protein

🥗 Ingredients

  • 50g oats
  • 250ml milk
  • 1 banana
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter

👨‍🍳 Preparation

Cook oats with milk for a few minutes. Add sliced banana and peanut butter. Mix well and serve.

🍛

Dal Rice

Cooked rice with dal and mixed vegetables. A balanced everyday Indian meal.

🔥 550 kcal 💪 20g protein

🥗 Ingredients

  • 200g cooked rice
  • 150g cooked dal
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Spices
  • Salad

👨‍🍳 Preparation

Cook dal with spices and vegetables. Serve with cooked rice and fresh salad.

🧀

Paneer Bowl

Paneer with vegetables and rice. A protein-rich meal for active lifestyles.

🔥 500 kcal 💪 28g protein

🥗 Ingredients

  • 100g paneer
  • 150g cooked rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Spices
  • 1 teaspoon oil

👨‍🍳 Preparation

Cut paneer into small pieces. Cook vegetables with spices and lightly sauté the paneer. Serve with rice.

🥤

Banana Shake

Milk, banana and peanut butter shake. Great as a quick energy-rich snack.

🔥 400 kcal 💪 14g protein

🥗 Ingredients

  • 250ml milk
  • 1 large banana
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • Ice cubes

👨‍🍳 Preparation

Add milk, banana and peanut butter into a blender. Blend until smooth. Add ice and serve chilled.

🥪

Healthy Veg Sandwich

Whole wheat bread with vegetables and paneer for a nutritious snack.

🔥 350 kcal 💪 16g protein

🥗 Ingredients

  • 4 slices whole wheat bread
  • 50g paneer
  • Tomato
  • Cucumber
  • Onion
  • Green chutney

👨‍🍳 Preparation

Spread green chutney on the bread. Add paneer and sliced vegetables. Toast lightly and serve fresh.

🫘

High Protein Soya Bowl

Soya chunks with vegetables and rice. A budget-friendly high-protein meal.

🔥 480 kcal 💪 30g protein

🥗 Ingredients

  • 50g dry soya chunks
  • 150g cooked rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Spices
  • 1 teaspoon oil

👨‍🍳 Preparation

Boil soya chunks and squeeze out excess water. Cook with vegetables and spices. Serve with cooked rice.
